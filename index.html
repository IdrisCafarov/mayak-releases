First time opening it

Mayak is free and isn't sold through an app store, so your computer shows a one-time warning. Here's exactly what to click.

Windows

  1. Open the downloaded Mayak…setup.exe.
  2. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears, click More info:

    Windows protected your PC

    Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting. More info
    Don't run
  3. Then click Run anyway:

    Windows protected your PC

    App: Mayak_setup.exe
    Publisher: Unknown publisher
    Run anywayDon't run
  4. The installer adds Mayak to your Start menu and desktop. No admin rights needed.

Mac

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Mayak onto Applications.
  2. Open Mayak from Applications. If macOS says it “can't be opened”, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway:
    Security
    “Mayak” was blocked to protect your Mac.Open Anyway
  4. Confirm once more. From now on Mayak opens normally.
